REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Enterprise Customer Engagement Platform
RFP No. CEP-2026-014
Issued by: Meridian Financial Services Corp.
Primary Contact: Jordan Blake, Procurement Manager
Email: jordan.blake@meridianfinancial.example
Phone: 312-555-0147

1. Purpose
Meridian Financial Services Corp. seeks a qualified vendor to implement and support an
enterprise customer engagement platform that integrates with existing systems of record,
meets regulated-industry compliance obligations, and can be deployed within the stated
implementation timeline and budget envelope.

2. Technical Requirements
2.1 The solution shall provide a documented REST API integration capability with OAuth 2.0
authentication, webhook eventing, and support for near-real-time data exchange with CRM
and core banking applications.
2.2 The platform must meet a minimum monthly uptime SLA of 99.9%, measured excluding
mutually agreed maintenance windows, with service credits defined in the MSA.
2.3 All customer data at rest must be encrypted using AES-256 (or equivalent) and all data
in transit must use TLS 1.2 or higher.

3. Compliance Requirements
3.1 The vendor must maintain a current SOC 2 Type II certification covering security,
availability, and confidentiality trust service criteria, and provide the latest report under NDA.
3.2 Customer data processed under this engagement must support United States data
residency controls; production data shall not leave US regions without prior written approval.

4. Timeline Requirements
4.1 Full production implementation must be completed no later than December 31, 2026.
4.2 The vendor shall deliver a detailed project plan with milestones for discovery, pilot,
and production cutover no later than Q3 2026, including a pilot go-live target of
September 15, 2026.

5. Budget Constraints
5.1 Total first-year cost of ownership (licenses, implementation, and support) shall not
exceed $1,250,000.00 USD, exclusive of pre-approved change orders.

6. Staffing Requirements
6.1 The vendor shall assign a dedicated account manager (or Customer Success Manager)
as the single point of contact for commercial and service escalations.
6.2 The vendor must provide on-site technical support for Severity-1 incidents at the
Meridian Chicago headquarters within four (4) hours of incident declaration during
business days.

7. Submission Instructions
Proposals are due by 5:00 PM CT on October 1, 2026. Late submissions will not be considered.
