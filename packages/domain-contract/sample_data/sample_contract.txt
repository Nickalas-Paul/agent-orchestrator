MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — SampleCorp / Acme Vendor

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026
between SampleCorp ("Client") and Acme Vendor LLC ("Provider").

Section 3.1 — Limitation of Liability
Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall
not exceed the fees paid by Client to Provider during the twelve (12) months preceding
the claim. IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL,
CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY
OF LIABILITY.

Section 4.2 — Intellectual Property
All work product, deliverables, and materials developed under this Agreement shall be
owned by the party that creates them, subject to Provider retaining a non-exclusive,
royalty-free license to use generic methodologies and residual knowledge. Client is
granted a license to use deliverables solely for its internal business purposes.
Ownership of jointly developed materials is not expressly assigned.

Section 5.1 — Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior
written notice to the other party. Upon termination, Client shall pay for Services
performed through the effective date.

Section 6.3 — Confidentiality
Each party agrees to protect the other party's Confidential Information for a period
of two (2) years following disclosure, using at least the same degree of care it uses
to protect its own confidential information of like importance.

Section 7.2 — Indemnification
Provider shall indemnify Client against third-party claims arising solely from
Provider's willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing the Services. This
indemnification does not extend to intellectual property infringement claims, product
liability, or claims arising from Client-supplied materials.

Section 8.1 — Data Handling
Provider shall implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and
physical safeguards designed to protect Client Data. Provider is not obligated to
maintain specific certifications unless separately agreed in a statement of work.

Section 9.1 — Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard
to conflict-of-law principles. Disputes shall first be submitted to good-faith
negotiation for fifteen (15) days before either party may pursue litigation.

Section 10.4 — Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond
its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, labor disputes,
or failures of third-party infrastructure, provided the affected party gives prompt
notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.
