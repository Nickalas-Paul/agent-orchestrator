PREFERRED / STANDARD CONTRACT TERMS — Procurement Playbook

1. Liability Cap
Preferred: Unlimited liability for IP infringement, confidentiality breaches, and
willful misconduct. For all other claims, cap at twenty-four (24) months of fees paid.
Indirect and consequential damages may be excluded except for confidentiality and IP.

2. Termination Notice
Preferred: Sixty (60) days' prior written notice for termination for convenience.
Immediate termination permitted for material breach uncured within thirty (30) days.

3. Intellectual Property Ownership
Preferred: Client owns all deliverables, work product, and custom developments upon
payment. Provider retains only pre-existing tools and generic methodologies with no
license over Client confidential information.

4. Confidentiality Duration
Preferred: Five (5) years from disclosure, or indefinitely for trade secrets.

5. Indemnification Scope
Preferred: Broad indemnification covering third-party claims for IP infringement,
personal injury, property damage, data breaches caused by Provider, and negligence —
not limited to willful misconduct or gross negligence.

6. Data Protection
Preferred: Provider maintains SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent) and complies with GDPR/CCPA
as applicable. Specific administrative, technical, and physical controls must be
documented; "commercially reasonable" alone is insufficient.

7. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
Preferred: Delaware law with binding arbitration under AAA rules after a thirty (30)
day negotiation period.

8. Force Majeure
Preferred: Excludes payment obligations; requires prompt notice within five (5)
business days and mitigation efforts; does not excuse prolonged outages beyond sixty
(60) days without termination rights for the non-affected party.
